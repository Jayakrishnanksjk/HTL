April 1996

Foundational Milestone

Founded in 1996 by Mr. Lavinder Duggal as Magnum Air Technics, a sole proprietorship pioneered HVAC excellence as a trusted dealer and contractor for major AC brands.

April 2004

Transition to HTL Aircon Pvt. Ltd. & Partnership with Daikin

HTL Aircon transitioned to a Private Limited entity and forged a pivotal alliance with Daikin, strengthening its leadership in innovative, high-performance VRV system solutions.

April 2010

Expansion to Pune: Strengthening Western Operations

HTL expanded its footprint with a new Pune branch, reinforcing its Mumbai base to enhance regional project management and deliver seamless, high-quality HVAC solutions across Western India.

April 2014

Expansion to Delhi NCR: Strengthening Presence in the North

In 2010, HTL established its Delhi NCR branch, enabling efficient project execution and seamless operations across Northern India.

April 2016

Southern Expansion: Strengthening HTL’s Pan-India Presence

HTL launched branches in Hyderabad and Bengaluru, boosting growth in the industrial and GCC segments while strengthening South India's project execution capabilities.

April 2018

Launch of Turnkey MEP Contracting

Transitioning from a top 5 HVAC player in the West, HTL evolved into a comprehensive turnkey MEP contractor, delivering integrated MEP solutions, setting new benchmarks in project execution and client value.

April 2020

Inroads in Data Centre

HTL entered the Data Centre sector through specialized testing and commissioning expertise, rapidly advancing to deliver full turnkey solutions, showcasing our capability in mission-critical, high-efficiency infrastructure projects.

April 2023

International Expansion

HTL emerged among Foxconn’s first MEP partners in India, delivering its largest industrial project in Bengaluru. The Dubai office marked our global footprint, extending operations across the MENA region.

April 2024

Strategic Acquisition Drives Pharma Leadership

HTL’s acquisition of Psychometrics significantly enhanced its engineering prowess, enabling a swift and authoritative entry into the regulated pharmaceutical and life sciences sector with advanced cleanroom and process engineering solutions.

April 2025

Launching HTL Biopharma—Dedicated Vertical for Highly Regulated Facilities

HTL launched its Biopharma vertical, reinforcing commitment to innovation and excellence in life sciences. Abhay Ranjan’s appointment as CEO drives visionary leadership and growth in this highly regulated sector.
