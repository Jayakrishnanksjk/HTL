Leadership

HTL began with Lavinder Duggal’s founding vision, to build an engineering-driven organisation rooted in integrity and long-term trust. With Bhupinder Duggal’s strategic leadership, that vision has matured into a future-ready company known for precision, reliability, and enduring client partnerships. Together, they’ve nurtured a people-first culture that values craftsmanship, accountability, and lasting relationships.
